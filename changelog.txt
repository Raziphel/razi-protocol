  Balancing:
    - cross-planet exchange recipes take productivity up to +200%; without it the electromagnetic plant made more with the normal recipe than with the exchange
    - huge asteroids on space routes only from route level 9, where railguns are within reach; Khemia - Ribbonia, Aquilo - Balack and Beetlejuice - Umbra had them from about level 6 ([#240](https://github.com/Raziphel/razi-protocol/issues/240))

    - recommend the Even Distribution fork by Jhorr, which keeps quality and freshness on Ctrl+drag and takes over the settings of the original
    - the description of the Easy, Hard and Nightmare map presets says why their sliders stay at 100 % when the mod setting picks the same difficulty
    - the game starts with Lithium Battery and Vesta without its legacy recipes
    - the game starts with Ribbonia when the Renai Transportation bounce plates, throwers or train ramps were turned off; these settings are pinned on
    - the original Even Distribution is incompatible: its Factorio 2.1 release crashes when a game starts (fuel_category was removed in 2.1.20)
    - [weave] ignore Nexus omega labs for campaign science ([#247](https://github.com/Raziphel/razi-protocol/issues/247))
