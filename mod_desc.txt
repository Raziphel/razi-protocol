# Razi Protocol

I made Razi Protocol for the kind of Space Age playthrough where you install far too many planets and then realise half of them barely interact. It's built around Factorio 2.1 and Krastorio 2 Spaced Out, with the idea of turning those separate mods into one long expedition.

The starmap, technology tree and factory progression are tied together. You still get the original planets, machines and odd little mechanics that make each mod worth playing, but now there's a reason to bring materials and research between them.

## The Galaxy

The seven main systems follow three pairs of increasingly difficult expeditions:

- **Calidus:** Nauvis, Muluna, your first platform fleet and the Dyson Sphere project.
- **Solaris:** Vulcanus, Castra, Arig, Hyarion, Tellus, Corrundum and their industrial neighbours.
- **Nyxaris:** Gleba, Moshine, Apia/Carnova, Panglia and the biological frontier.
- **Vibrant:** Fulgora, Cerys, Ribbonia, Paracelsin and the asteroid crossing.
- **Vespera:** Muria, Aquilo, Rubia, Maraxsis and the colder outer routes.
- **Beetlejuice:** Cubium, Linox, Secretas and Frozeta.
- **Umbra:** Tenebris, Crucible, Vesta and the farthest factory challenges.

Solaris and Nyxaris can be started independently. So can the later paired systems, once you've finished the previous tier. The Aegis/Bellicos expedition, the solar-system edge and Deep Space are their own longer-distance projects.

Some locations and features come from optional add-ons. Check `info.json` for the current required list.

## Research and Manufacturing

System tech cards hold the main research chapters together. Existing planet sciences still matter, and the first trip to a planet shouldn't need its own science pack.

There's also a growing network of alternate recipes for people who like building cargo routes. Moshine optics, Paracelsin zinc, Linox rare earths, Rubian composites, Vesta magnets and K2SO components all have places where they can help each other. These are bonus production options, not shortcuts past the original research.

The asteroid belts and major travel routes have their own progression. The map shows where you can go next without turning every nearby planet into a dead end.

## The Protocol Codex

The in-game Codex catalogs 228 conditional research and exploration milestones across 18 chapters. You can search the objectives, browse science and recipe unlocks, pin a goal, read recovered transmissions and track how long each part of your run took.

Each chapter has its own colours, art and atmosphere. Open it from the small top-row button or press **Ctrl + Shift + J**.

## More to Explore

- The native K2SO intergalactic transceiver opens Deep Space. The real ending is a five-stage, physically delivered Convergence megaproject tied to all seven systems, the Calidus Dyson works and deep-space industry. The last shipment starts a save-safe final transmission before the native victory screen.
- A Calidus Dyson Sphere project with staged construction.
- Galactic Operations for navigating a crowded planet and platform list, with an integrated Convergence console, partial construction meters and native Deep Space expedition briefings.
- A custom starmap with better local connections, route descriptions and space scenery.
- Curated enemy encounters, including optional enemy mods on the planets that suit them.
- A dedicated Vehicles & Infrastructure crafting tab and tidier science groups.
- An optional Discord Rich Presence companion.
- Nexus, bosses, Celestial Weather and other supported integrations where installed.

Most of the pack is controlled through startup settings. Some settings change research or recipes, so back up a save before switching an ongoing campaign.
