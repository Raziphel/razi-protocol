## Welcome to Razi Protocol 3.0

Razi Protocol turns a huge collection of Space Age planet mods into one connected expedition. It's built around interstellar exploration, meaningful planetary research, specialized factories and the logistics needed to keep them all working together.

Your journey starts in Calidus and grows into six more star systems. Each system has a central **Tech Card** that ties its planets and their research together. Discovering another world isn't the end of the previous one: its materials, technology and industry remain part of the wider campaign.

There is plenty to do between launches. The **Expedition Journal** follows your quests, milestones and construction projects, while curated space routes give the galaxy a structure you can plan around. Eventually, the Dyson Sphere, the transceiver and the challenges of Deep Space lead to a real, construction-driven finale at **Convergence**.

Razi Protocol doesn't try to replace the mods it brings together. Their unique planets and mechanics remain intact. The goal is to make them feel like parts of the same journey.

## Please Read Before Starting

- Razi Protocol changes progression from the beginning of the game, not just after Space Age's usual endgame.
- **Start a new save for the intended 3.0 experience.** Back up existing saves before testing major updates.
- Space Age, Krastorio 2 Spaced Out and the required companion mods form the campaign. Check the Dependencies tab before installing.
- **Normal** is the designed starting difficulty. Easy, Hard and Nightmare offer different challenges, with additional settings for finer control.
- Some options change research, recipes or world generation. Startup-setting changes require a game restart, and existing terrain is not regenerated.
- Every system has a role beyond its first discovery. Expect to ship materials and maintain factories across multiple worlds.
- The built-in **Expedition Journal** helps track objectives, projects and your next steps. Open it with **Ctrl + Shift + J**.
- Razi Protocol 3.0 is actively being developed. Progression, balance and compatibility may change before release.

## Razi Protocol Is for You If

- You want a much larger, more interconnected Space Age campaign.
- You enjoy complex science trees where planets contribute to shared system-wide research.
- You want factories on older worlds to stay useful long after you move on.
- You like planning dangerous routes, cargo networks and specialized interplanetary industries.
- You want actual projects to build, not just another technology to research.
- You enjoy adjusting enemies, asteroid hazards, research, visuals and performance to your playstyle.
- You want an expedition with a beginning, a middle and an end worth working toward.

## Explore the galaxy

- **Calidus:** Nauvis, Muluna and the Dyson Sphere.
- **Solaris & Nyxaris:** industrial and biological frontiers.
- **Vibrant & Vespera:** electricity, cold worlds and asteroid belts.
- **Beetlejuice & Umbra:** demanding outer-system expeditions.
- **Beyond:** Bellicos, Aegis, Deep Space and the Convergence finale.

Paired systems offer route choices without skipping the intended research progression. Original planet mechanics remain central.

## Your expedition

**Research with purpose.** Seven system science chapters connect existing planetary technologies. Optional cross-world recipes reward building real shipping networks without replacing native production.

**A living Journal.** Follow objectives, recovered transmissions, chapter stories, factory projects and milestone records. Hide spoilers, pin goals or just explore. Open the Journal with **Ctrl + Shift + J**.

**A readable starmap.** Curated routes, ten-level danger colors, useful travel tooltips and customizable sky details help you choose where to go next.

**One campaign victory, earned at Convergence.** Space Age, K2SO and legacy rocket victory screens are suppressed through their supported APIs while their gameplay remains intact. **A genuine physical endgame.** Unlock Deep Space through the K2SO transceiver, assemble five increasingly demanding cargo Mandates, defeat all nine Nexus bosses through their real summon encounters, build and power a seven-star witness network and a 50 MW expedition Array Core, physically visit every reachable Deep Space destination, then complete the two-minute Convergence finale. Real logistics and physical engineering reveal the dark truth behind RAZI's lost executive order.

## Make it yours

Mod settings let you tune asteroid populations, industry links, research distribution, map visuals, performance experiments, Journal controls and more. The standard defaults preserve the designed campaign; risky performance options are disabled by default.

Startup settings may change existing progression or recipes. **Back up your save before switching campaign modes.**
