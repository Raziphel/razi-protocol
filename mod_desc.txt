# Welcome to Razi Protocol 3.0

Razi Protocol is a large Space Age campaign built around connected star systems, planetary progression, interstellar logistics, and the technologies needed to push ever farther into space.

The journey begins on Nauvis and grows across seven star systems. Each system brings together several distinct worlds and their own industries, with a shared Tech Card linking their research. Instead of treating each planet as a one-time science stop, Razi Protocol makes older factories, materials, and production networks valuable throughout the campaign.

A built-in Expedition Journal tracks quests, milestones, construction projects, and your progress through the galaxy. Curated space routes give exploration a clear direction while leaving you choices about where to travel, what to build, and how to move resources between worlds.

Eventually, your expedition moves beyond the familiar systems. The Dyson Sphere, the Transceiver, dangerous deep-space destinations, and the Convergence finale all demand more than another research unlock. You'll need working factories, real infrastructure, and a supply network capable of supporting the journey.

Razi Protocol builds on the original planet and content mods rather than replacing their unique mechanics. The goal is to make an enormous collection of worlds feel like one cohesive adventure.

# Please Read Before Starting

* Razi Protocol changes progression from the beginning of the game, not just after the usual Space Age endgame.
* A new save is strongly recommended for the intended 3.0 experience.
* Space Age, Krastorio 2 Spaced Out, and the required companion mods are part of the campaign. Check the Dependencies tab before installing.
* Normal difficulty is the intended experience. Easy, Hard, and Nightmare are also available.
* Many aspects of the experience can be adjusted in the settings, including threats, resource balance, asteroids, research, navigation, and visuals.
* Some settings require restarting Factorio. Changes to world generation do not regenerate existing terrain.
* Older planets remain part of the wider progression after their first research milestones are complete.
* The Expedition Journal helps explain your next objectives and projects. Open it with Ctrl + Shift + J.
* Razi Protocol 3.0 is in active development. Progression, balance, and compatibility may change between versions.

# Razi Protocol Is for You If

* You want a much larger and more interconnected Space Age playthrough.
* You enjoy complex technology trees and system-wide science progression.
* You like building specialized factories across multiple planets instead of abandoning each one after unlocking its science.
* You want interplanetary logistics, hazardous routes, and meaningful industrial dependencies.
* You enjoy long-term construction goals, dangerous expeditions, and a physical endgame.
* You want to customize difficulty, combat, resources, space travel, and galaxy visuals to suit your playstyle.

## Explore the galaxy

- **Calidus:** Nauvis, Muluna and the Dyson Sphere.
- **Solaris & Nyxaris:** industrial and biological frontiers.
- **Vibrant & Vespera:** electricity, cold worlds and asteroid belts.
- **Beetlejuice & Umbra:** demanding outer-system expeditions.
- **Beyond:** Bellicos, Aegis, Deep Space and the Convergence finale.

Paired systems offer route choices without skipping the intended research progression. Original planet mechanics remain central.

## Your expedition

**Research with purpose.** Seven system science chapters connect existing planetary technologies. Optional cross-world recipes reward building real shipping networks without replacing native production.

**A living Journal.** Follow objectives, recovered transmissions, chapter stories, factory projects and milestone records. Hide spoilers, pin goals or just explore. Open the Journal with **Ctrl + Shift + J**.

**A readable starmap.** Curated routes, ten-level danger colors, useful travel tooltips and customizable sky details help you choose where to go next.

**One campaign victory, earned at Convergence.** Space Age, K2SO and legacy rocket victory screens are suppressed through their supported APIs while their gameplay remains intact. **A genuine physical endgame.** Unlock Deep Space through the K2SO transceiver, assemble five increasingly demanding cargo Mandates, defeat all nine Nexus bosses through their real summon encounters, build and power a seven-star witness network and a 50 MW expedition Array Core, physically visit every reachable Deep Space destination, then complete the two-minute Convergence finale. Real logistics and physical engineering reveal the dark truth behind RAZI's lost executive order.

## Make it yours

Mod settings let you tune asteroid populations, industry links, research distribution, map visuals, performance experiments, Journal controls and more. The standard defaults preserve the designed campaign; risky performance options are disabled by default.

Startup settings may change existing progression or recipes. **Back up your save before switching campaign modes.**
