# Razi Protocol

**One galaxy. One connected expedition.**

Razi Protocol brings sprawling **Factorio 2.1 + Krastorio 2 Spaced Out** planet packs together into a coherent campaign. Instead of a collection of isolated worlds, discoveries, science and manufacturing build on each other across seven star systems.

## Explore the galaxy

- **Calidus:** Nauvis, Muluna and the Dyson Sphere.
- **Solaris & Nyxaris:** industrial and biological frontiers.
- **Vibrant & Vespera:** electricity, cold worlds and asteroid belts.
- **Beetlejuice & Umbra:** demanding outer-system expeditions.
- **Beyond:** Bellicos, Aegis, Deep Space and the Convergence finale.

Paired systems offer route choices without skipping the intended research progression. Original planet mechanics remain central.

## Your expedition

**Research with purpose.** Seven system science chapters connect existing planetary technologies. Optional cross-world recipes reward building real shipping networks without replacing native production.

**A living Journal.** Follow objectives, recovered transmissions, chapter stories, factory projects and milestone records. Hide spoilers, pin goals or just explore. Open the Journal with **Ctrl + Shift + J**.

**A readable starmap.** Curated routes, ten-level danger colors, useful travel tooltips and customizable sky details help you choose where to go next.

**One campaign victory, earned at Convergence.** Space Age, K2SO and legacy rocket victory screens are suppressed through their supported APIs while their gameplay remains intact. **A genuine physical endgame.** Unlock Deep Space through the K2SO transceiver, assemble five increasingly demanding cargo Mandates, defeat all nine Nexus bosses through their real summon encounters, build and power a seven-star witness network and a 50 MW expedition Array Core, physically visit every reachable Deep Space destination, then complete the two-minute Convergence finale. Real logistics and physical engineering reveal the dark truth behind RAZI's lost executive order.

## Make it yours

Mod settings let you tune asteroid populations, industry links, research distribution, map visuals, performance experiments, Journal controls and more. The standard defaults preserve the designed campaign; risky performance options are disabled by default.

Startup settings may change existing progression or recipes. **Back up your save before switching campaign modes.**
