# Interplanetary industry: exchange design playbook

**Principle:** Every optional exchange needs a reason to exist beyond paying
for another research pack. It should create a *logistics decision* that can be
seen in the recipe, work with the native process, and make an upstream world
worth revisiting.

This portfolio includes 15 guarded native-K2SO/planet-mod alternatives,
selected from the former 58 overlapping optional variants.
Missing source mods or ingredients suppress individual alternatives. Nothing
overwrites third-party recipes or gates base planetary discoveries behind
optional exchange research.

## Three actual manufacturing benefits

| Process | Mechanical benefit | Industrial explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Catalytic | Saves up to 12% of one named native ingredient, rounded down per recipe; imported cargo still consumed | Reagents, improved dopants, lower scrap and reduced rejects |
| Accelerated | Faster batch time, but target product output per machine-second cannot exceed 117% of the native process | Imported controls, measurement and precision equipment |
| Bulk | Keeps source batch pacing and uses freight materials for modest extra output | Scaled manufacturing, reinforced tools and stable mass production |

Deep increases **imported** inputs by 50% (rounded up), never prior-star card
costs. Native input savings, research, source conditions and output caps do not
change between modes. No automatic recycling or productivity stacking.

required source mods and production paths are present.

## Safeguards and review checklist

1. Source recipe and output must still exist, be enabled by valid native
   research, and be craftable on the intended surface and machine.
2. Every advertised catalyst must match a real native ingredient; a missing
   one **skips that optional recipe**, not the native one.
3. No exchange may produce more than its bounded gross-output bonus. Dynamic
   accelerated throughput is capped at +17% versus the original machine
   seconds, with no bonus productivity or recycling.
4. Supplier unlocks and their producer inputs must still exist.
5. Never add tech-card costs just to create fake interdependence. Research may
   use ordinary science; real planet arrivals and physical material flows
   control the industrial graph.
6. Before adding an exchange, identify a **different output**, a **different
   factory bottleneck relieved**, or a **new return-cargo use**. Do not add a
   second recipe that does the same job on the same planet.
7. Audit the actual installed modpack before balancing numbers: compatibility
   checks cannot prove that cargo is economical, nor that all optional
   source worlds exist in a specific game.
